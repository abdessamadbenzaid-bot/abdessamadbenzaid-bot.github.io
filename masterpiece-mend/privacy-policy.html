Privacy Policy

Masterpiece Mend: Art Puzzle (Android, package ma.samed.masterpiecemend) · Effective 25 November 2026

Masterpiece Mend is made by Samed (Abdessamad Benzaid), an independent developer in Khemisset, Morocco ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the game uses and why. In short: the game itself collects no personal data; ads are provided by Google AdMob, which uses some device data to show and measure ads, with your consent where the law requires it.

1. Data the game itself handles

  • No account, no sign-in. You never give me your name, email, phone number or contacts.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. Finished levels, stars and the sound setting are saved only in the app's local storage on your device. I cannot see them. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them.
  • No location, camera, microphone, photos or files. The game does not ask for these permissions.
  • Offline play. The paintings, fonts and game are inside the app. The internet is used only to show ads and to open a museum page if you tap its link (it opens in your browser).

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad sometimes after you finish a puzzle (never during play), and an optional video you can choose to watch for a hint. To serve and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in your Android settings),
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it (not GPS),
  • ad interactions (views, clicks) and diagnostic data such as crash logs from the ads SDK.

Google uses this data as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Ads in the game are limited to a general-audience content rating.

3. Consent and your choices

  • Europe (EEA), UK and Switzerland: on first launch the game shows Google's consent message (a certified consent tool). You can accept, refuse or choose purposes. If you refuse personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads.
  • United States: where state privacy laws apply, you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising through the same message.
  • Change your mind at any time: open the Gallery → Image credits → Privacy settings in the game (shown where the law requires it).
  • On any Android phone: Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads) lets you delete or reset your advertising ID.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

Personalised advertising relies on your consent. Non-personalised ads and the security of the service rely on legitimate interest in keeping a free game available. Local progress saving is needed to provide the game you asked for.

5. Children

Masterpiece Mend is intended for adults and is not directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has used the game and data was shared with the ad provider, contact me and I will help you make a request to Google.

6. Sharing, sale and retention

I do not sell your data and I do not receive personal data from the game. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is kept by Google under its own retention rules. Data is sent over encrypted connections.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal data, and to complain to your data protection authority. Since I hold no personal data about you, most requests concern Google's ad data; I will gladly point you to the right place.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
